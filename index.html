I'm a master's student in Computer Science at Johns Hopkins University, working on embodied AI and robot learning for manipulation. At JHU I work with Prof. Tianmin Shu (SCAI Lab) on simulation benchmarks and data generation for part-level manipulation, and with Prof. Chien-Ming Huang (Intuitive Computing Lab) on long-term human–robot interaction. Previously, I trained and evaluated humanoid manipulation policies at a robotics startup in Berkeley, and worked on quadruped perception at Ant Group.

My interests: robot learning, scalable simulation & data engines, multimodal perception, and human–robot interaction.

PartInstruct 2.0: Part-Level Robot Manipulation Benchmark
In preparation · SCAI Lab, JHU · with Prof. Tianmin Shu

An Isaac Sim benchmark for fine-grained part-level manipulation with articulated assets, compositional tasks, multiple embodiments, and sim-to-real evaluation. I build the scalable data-generation pipeline: domain randomization, grasp generation, motion planning, and control.
